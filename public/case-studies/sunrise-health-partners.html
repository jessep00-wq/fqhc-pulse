01 — The challenge

Great QI work. No way to prove the financial return.

Sunrise Health Partners has operated as an FQHC for 14 years, serving nearly 20,000 patients across four suburban sites. Their QI program was active — they ran regular PDSA cycles, held monthly QI committee meetings, and tracked their UDS measures diligently.

But when it came to making the case for additional resources, renewing their HRSA grant, or negotiating quality-based payments with their Medicaid managed care partners, they had a persistent problem: their QI data lived in a different world from their financial data. The CFO couldn't see how a PDSA cycle on Hypertension Control translated into a dollar figure. The board couldn't understand why the QI budget was the right size. And during contract negotiations with their Medicaid MCO, Sunrise had no structured evidence package to show that their clinical improvement work was generating financial value.

"

We were doing real improvement work. But every time someone asked "what's the return on QI," we had nothing concrete to show them. The clinical data and the financial data lived in completely separate worlds.

Dr. Anita Roshan — CMO, Sunrise Health Partners

The consequence was predictable: QI was seen as a compliance function rather than a revenue driver. The QI Director, Kwame Asante, was skilled and committed — but without tools to translate his team's work into financial language, he was always fighting for budget rather than earning it.

02 — The approach

Making every PDSA cycle financially visible

Sunrise onboarded MeasureWise in September 2024. From the outset, Asante worked with the CFO to map each major UDS measure to its financial implications — identifying which measures were tied to MCO quality bonuses, which were weighted in HRSA's performance review, and which were linked to value-based payment thresholds.

1
Financial mapping

Each of Sunrise's 18 tracked UDS measures tagged with its revenue implications: HRSA scoring weight, MCO bonus threshold, and any value-based care program tie-in. This created a prioritized QI agenda based on financial impact, not just clinical urgency.

2
PDSA alignment

All active PDSA cycles linked to their corresponding measures. AI insights surfaced that 60% of active cycles were targeting measures with no direct financial tie-in — allowing Asante to reallocate QI resources to higher-return priorities.

3
Evidence package generation

MeasureWise generated structured QI progress reports formatted for HRSA review and MCO contract negotiation. For the first time, Sunrise could hand an external stakeholder a document showing specific PDSA cycles tied to specific measure improvements tied to specific financial outcomes.

4
Board-level visibility

Monthly QI dashboards sent directly to board members showed the relationship between improvement activity and financial performance — transforming QI from a compliance checkbox into a board-level strategic discussion.
