The challenge

Buried in spreadsheets, starved for insight

Northside Family Health Alliance operates six sites across a major Midwest city, serving 28,000 patients annually — one of the largest FQHCs in the region. The QI department consisted of two full-time coordinators: Jamila Crawford, QI Director, and her colleague Terrence Bautista, QI Specialist.

On paper, Northside had a well-functioning QI program. In practice, Crawford and Bautista were spending the equivalent of nearly an entire workweek every month just managing information — updating spreadsheets, formatting reports for leadership, chasing status updates from site leads, and reconciling data across four different tracking systems. By the time they finished the monthly QI board report, half the data was stale.

The approach

Consolidation, automation, and focus

Crawford and Bautista migrated all active PDSA documentation into MeasureWise over a two-week period. The process surfaced an immediate problem: Northside had 47 active PDSA cycles across six sites, but only 12 of them were directly tied to UDS measures with performance flags. The other 35 were improvement projects with no clear connection to the health center's most critical metrics.

Using MeasureWise's AI insights layer, the team identified which cycles were highest priority given Northside's UDS profile. Three measures stood out: Cervical Cancer Screening (31% below national average), Tobacco Use Screening (flagged in prior SAC review), and Weight Assessment in Children (below 40%). Crawford restructured the QI calendar around these three, reassigning resources from lower-priority cycles.

The change that surprised the team most was the weekly QI snapshot email. Every Monday morning, each site lead received a short automated summary of their active cycles — what phase each was in, what was due that week, and which UDS measures were trending in the right direction. For the first time, site leads felt ownership over QI progress without needing to attend an additional meeting or log into a new tool.

Northside participates in a Medicaid managed care quality bonus program tied to specific clinical measures, including Cervical Cancer Screening and Tobacco Use Screening. Bringing those measures above threshold levels unlocked bonus payments that had been missed in the prior contract year.
