## Rules
- If they reply at any point, **stop the sequence** and respond like a human.
- If they don't reply after DM 3, move to "quarterly check-in" — like a post they make, comment once every 6–8 weeks, no more DMs.
- Never send a calendar link in DM 1 or DM 2. Earn it.
